What data we process

  • Messages and chat history: When you use the chat, your messages and the AI’s replies may be sent to our API (hosted on Vercel) and to third‑party AI providers (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek) to generate answers. Chat history may be stored temporarily in your browser (localStorage) and is not stored on our servers unless the hosting provider keeps short-lived logs.
  • Attachments: If you upload an image or PDF, that content is sent to our API and may be sent to AI providers for processing. We do not use it for training.
  • Technical data: The hosting provider (e.g. Vercel) may log IP address, user agent, and request metadata for security and operation.

Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your data on the basis of contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) and, where applicable, our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for operating and securing the service.

Retention

We do not retain your chat content on our own systems. Data in your browser (localStorage) stays until you clear it. Provider logs are kept only as long as required by the provider’s policy or law.
